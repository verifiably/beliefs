The acquisition-boundary predicate intentionally does not confer empirical standing on produced datasets through lineage. Define whether and how standing can be inherited, including evidence closure, aliases, unavailable producers and deletion/retraction effects, before changing eligibility. Preserve the current acquisition bearer rule until a design is agreed.
