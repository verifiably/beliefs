Raised by the user 2026-09-26 while reviewing the guide overview. Proto-science's pre-registrations often became costume: arm-waved claims and ungrounded parameter estimates, amended after looking at the data. Direction: put more weight on observation — systems and strategies that characterize datasets of different types and modalities through simple, well-grounded methods (statistical, information-theoretic, unbiased detectors), minimizing human and AI decisions in early processing and detection; then weigh one model or several (equally, or with data-based priors) by what is seen. Today's kernel path starts from a stated proposition and an analysis spec frozen before the run (G4 keeps recorded failed attempts visible).
