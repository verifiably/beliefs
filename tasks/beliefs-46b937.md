Declaring a Zenodo dataset with locator doi:10.5281/zenodo.22399365 was refused: 'locator' uses scheme 'doi'; declared schemes are accession, url, instrument. The milestone wrote the DOI as its resolver URL (url:https://doi.org/...). Whether a DOI is its own scheme is a contract decision.
